Meet Agamemnon.

Our flagship antivirus for macOS and Windows. It doesn't just look up files it already knows: it watches what programs do and stops today's Mac stealers in the act. Free and open source.

Agamemnon

Serious protection that stays quiet until it matters.

Available now macOS 13+ Windows 10 & 11 GPL-2.0
  • Behaviour protectionWatches every new program and stops it the moment it fakes a password prompt, raids your keychain or browser data, or uploads your files. Catches brand-new variants that no fingerprint list has seen yet.
  • 1.1 million known threatsQuick, full, custom and scheduled scans against more than 1.1 million confirmed malware fingerprints from MalwareBazaar, refreshed every 12 hours. Add ClamAV for a second engine.
  • Download protectionNew files in Downloads are scanned as soon as they finish. Dangerous ones go straight to quarantine.
  • No hiding in archives, no zip bombsOpens ZIP, RAR, 7Z, ISO, DMG, PKG, MSI and more, three levels deep. Zip bombs are refused before a single byte is unpacked.
  • Encrypted DNSDNS-over-HTTPS or TLS with Cloudflare, Quad9, Mullvad, AdGuard and others, including malware-blocking resolvers.
  • Admin and startup alertsWarns you when an app asks for administrator rights or sets itself up to start automatically.
  • 1.1M+known malware fingerprints
  • 12 hbetween database updates
  • ~1 sto react to a new program
  • 0false alarms across ~750 apps in our tests

Built against the threats Mac users actually face.

Behaviour rules written for the malware families hitting Macs today, on top of the fingerprint database.

  • Stopped in the actAtomic Stealer (AMOS)Fake password prompts, keychain theft, AppleScript data grabs and uploads of your files.
  • Stopped in the actFrigidStealerThe fake browser update and the password and data theft that follows.
  • Stopped in the actCrypto minersMining-pool connections and known miner programs that eat your CPU.
  • Stopped in the actSysJokerThe backdoor posing as a macOS update, caught where it installs itself.
  • RefusedZip bombsArchives that would unpack past 4 GB or 100,000 files are rejected up front.
  • DetectedBeaverTail · KeyStealFake job-interview malware payloads and keychain stealers, plus startup-item alerts.

Our history.

October 2026. Athene Softworks was founded by Mert Yeşil and Miraz Bakış, two friends who wanted security software that is honest about what it does.

Agamemnon began as a research project on macOS and grew into a full antivirus for Mac and Windows. Platform365 followed. Today we build both in the open, and we're working on what comes next.
