Built against the threats people actually face.

Behaviour rules written for the malware families spreading today, on top of the fingerprint database.

  • Stopped in the actAMOS · FrigidStealerFake password prompts and browser updates, keychain theft and uploads of your files.
  • Stopped in the actBanshee · Cuckoo · PoseidonVirtual-machine checks, secret screenshots and wallet theft.
  • Stopped in the actRansomwareUnsigned programs that start scrambling your files are stopped within seconds.
  • Stopped in the actSysJoker · crypto minersThe fake macOS update backdoor and miners that eat your CPU.
  • Flagged for removalShlayer · Bundlore · AdloadAdware extensions and search hijacks, removed in one click.
  • DetectedBeaverTail · KeyStealFake job-interview malware payloads and keychain stealers, plus startup-item alerts.

Our history.

October 2026. Athene Softworks was founded by Mert Yeşil and Miraz Bakış, two friends who wanted security software that is honest about what it does.

Agamemnon began as a research project on macOS and grew into a full antivirus for Mac and Windows. Synapse followed. Today we build both in the open, and we're working on what comes next.
