What we do.

Meet Agamemnon.

Our first product. A free, open-source antivirus for macOS and Windows.

Agamemnon

Advanced antivirus, built to stay out of your way.

Available now macOS 13+ Windows 10 & 11 GPL-2.0
  • Malware scanningQuick, full, custom and scheduled scans, checked against more than a million known-malware hashes from MalwareBazaar, plus optional ClamAV.
  • Download protectionNew files in Downloads are scanned as soon as they finish. Dangerous ones go straight to quarantine.
  • Archive and installer scanningLooks inside ZIP, RAR, 7Z, ISO, DMG, PKG, MSI and more, up to three levels deep, with zip-bomb limits.
  • Encrypted DNSDNS-over-HTTPS or TLS with Cloudflare, Quad9, Mullvad, AdGuard and others, including malware-blocking resolvers.
  • Admin and startup alertsWarns you when an app asks for administrator rights or sets itself up to start automatically.

Our history.

October 2026. Athene Softworks was founded by Mert Yeşil and Miraz Bakış, two friends who wanted security software that is honest about what it does. Named after the goddess of wisdom, we name our products after the heroes of Greek myth.

Agamemnon started as a research project on macOS and quickly grew into a full antivirus with a Windows version of its own. Today we keep building it in the open, and we're working on what comes next.
