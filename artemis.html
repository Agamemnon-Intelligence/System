Artemis AI

Ask a general knowledge question. The model runs entirely in your browser.

Artemis 1.1

Hi, I'm Artemis.

A small general knowledge model made by Athene Softworks. It runs entirely on your device; nothing you type leaves your browser.

Artemis 2.0 Plasma is Artemis 1.0 and 1.1 merged into one 23.5M parameter model and trained further on Turkish and English conversations. Artemis 1.1 loads Artemis 1.0 (20M parameters) and adds its own 4.6M trained parameters on top. All three are small models that often make mistakes, so don't rely on their answers. Plasma Pro is built on Qwen3.5-0.8B, an open model from the Qwen team (Apache 2.0), with Artemis's identity added. It is a one-time download of about 500 MB and needs a browser with WebGPU. It is much stronger than the others but can still be wrong, especially in Turkish.

New Artemis 2.0 Plasma

Artemis 2.0 Plasma is here.

Plasma 2.0 brings Artemis 1.0 and 1.1 together in a single model and trains it further on Turkish and English conversations. It's the default model above, and it runs entirely in your browser.

  • 23.5Mparameters in a single model
  • 27layers: 1.0's 23 plus 1.1's 4
  • 24 MBone file instead of two
  • 0servers. Every answer is computed on your device
  • Built on Artemis 1.1 Artemis 1.1's additions are folded into the 1.0 base, so everything 1.1 learned is part of one model rather than a layer loaded on top.
  • More conversations Plasma 2.0 was trained on another 7.6 million tokens of Turkish and English conversations and Turkish Wikipedia.
  • One download The whole model is a single 24 MB file. It downloads once, and your browser keeps it for next time.
  • Private by design Like Artemis 1.x, nothing you type leaves your browser. There's no account, no log and no server to send it to.
  • Still a small model Plasma 2.0 often gets facts wrong and can drift off topic. Use it for fun and quick questions, and check anything important.
  • Free Plasma 2.0 is free to use on this page today, like every Artemis model before it.

New Artemis Plasma Pro

Artemis, with a much bigger brain.

Plasma Pro is built on Qwen3.5-0.8B, an open model from the Qwen team. We gave it Artemis's identity and its Turkish and English rules, and it still runs entirely in your browser. Try it above.

  • 0.8Bparameters, about 34× Plasma 2.0
  • GPUaccelerated on your graphics card through WebGPU
  • ~500 MBone-time download, kept by your browser
  • 0servers. Every answer is computed on your device
  • Built on Qwen Plasma Pro starts from Qwen3.5-0.8B, an open-weight model (Apache 2.0) trained on far more text than any earlier Artemis. That gives it much broader knowledge and far more fluent answers.
  • Artemis inside On top of Qwen we added Artemis's identity and rules: it introduces itself as Artemis by Athene Softworks, answers in the language you write in, and says so when it doesn't know.
  • Remembers the conversation Plasma Pro reads the recent turns of the chat with every question, so you can ask follow-ups without repeating yourself.
  • Private by design Like every Artemis model, nothing you type leaves your browser. There's no account, no log and no server to send it to.
  • Fast on your GPU Plasma Pro runs on your graphics card through WebGPU, which recent Chrome, Edge and Safari support. The model downloads once and your browser keeps it for next time. On a browser without WebGPU, Plasma 2.0 still works.
  • Free Plasma Pro is free to use on this page, like every Artemis model before it.

Plasma for organisations

Your own AI assistant. Your data stays with you.

Plasma for organisations is a Plasma Pro model trained on your own documents and running on your own systems. Nothing goes to an outside AI service.

  • Trained on your documents Guides, FAQs, policies and product information become a model that answers in your terms and introduces itself with your name.
  • Data stays in-house Questions and answers never leave your employees' devices. That makes the assistant much easier to fit under KVKK and GDPR than a cloud AI.
  • On your own network Plasma is a few static files. Put it on your intranet or ship it inside your own app, with no GPU servers to run and no per-question fees.
  • Works offline Once the model is loaded it needs no internet connection, so it works in the field, on closed networks and in the air.
  • Retrain whenever you need to When your documents change, the model is trained again on the new versions, in minutes on a single GPU.
  • Set up with Athene Softworks We prepare the data, train the model and help you put it in place, so your team gets a working assistant rather than a project.
