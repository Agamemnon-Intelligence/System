Artemis AI

Ask a general knowledge question. The model runs entirely in your browser.

Artemis 1.1

Hi, I'm Artemis.

A small general knowledge model made by Athene Softworks. It runs entirely on your device; nothing you type leaves your browser.

Artemis 1.1 loads Artemis 1.0 (20M parameters) and adds its own 4.6M trained parameters on top, so the two model files run together. Both are small models that often make mistakes, so don't rely on their answers. Artemis 2.0 Plasma is coming soon.

Coming soon Artemis 2.0 Plasma

A new Artemis, built from scratch.

Plasma 2.0 is not an update on top of 1.x. It's a new, larger model with its own Turkish and English vocabulary, and it still runs entirely in your browser.

  • 54Mparameters, 2.7× Artemis 1.0
  • 512tokens of context, twice as much as 1.x
  • 16Ktoken vocabulary built for Turkish and English
  • 0servers. Every answer is computed on your device
  • A modern architecture Rotary positions, normalised attention and a faster feed-forward layer, the same ideas used in today's large models, shrunk to fit a browser tab.
  • Turkish first A new vocabulary splits Turkish words into fewer, more meaningful pieces, so Plasma reads and writes Turkish more naturally than 1.x.
  • Remembers the conversation Plasma keeps what it has already read in memory, so a follow-up question starts answering right away instead of re-reading the whole chat.
  • Private by design Like Artemis 1.x, nothing you type leaves your browser. There's no account, no log and no server to send it to.
  • One file The whole model is a single file of about 55 MB. It downloads once, and your browser keeps it for next time.
  • Free Plasma 2.0 will be free to use on this page, like every Artemis model before it.

Plasma for organisations

Your own AI assistant. Your data stays with you.

Plasma for organisations is a Plasma 2.0 model trained on your own documents and running on your own systems. Nothing goes to an outside AI service.

  • Trained on your documents Guides, FAQs, policies and product information become a model that answers in your terms and introduces itself with your name.
  • Data stays in-house Questions and answers never leave your employees' devices. That makes the assistant much easier to fit under KVKK and GDPR than a cloud AI.
  • On your own network Plasma is a few static files. Put it on your intranet or ship it inside your own app, with no GPU servers to run and no per-question fees.
  • Works offline Once the model is loaded it needs no internet connection, so it works in the field, on closed networks and in the air.
  • Retrain whenever you need to When your documents change, the model is trained again on the new versions, in minutes on a single GPU.
  • Set up with Athene Softworks We prepare the data, train the model and help you put it in place, so your team gets a working assistant rather than a project.
