Platform365v3.0.0

An offline office suite for Windows and macOS. Present for slides and EasyWrite for documents, under one roof, compatible with PowerPoint and Word. No account, no server, no cloud: everything stays on your device.

Two apps, one suite.

Switch between them with the ▦ button. Both open and save the file formats you already use.

Present · Slides

Make and give presentations. Opens and saves PowerPoint (.pptx) and its own .present format.

  • Slides from text. Paste an outline: headings become slides, bullets become bullets, > lines become speaker notes. Long slides split by themselves.
  • Presentation health check. Contrast, text too small to read from the back, crowded slides, overflowing items and font chaos, scored out of 100.
  • Tools on stage. Pen, highlighter, laser pointer, magnifier, black or white screen, slide grid, a progress bar and elapsed time.
  • Live captions. What you say appears as subtitles at the bottom of the screen (Chrome and Edge).
  • Rehearsal mode. Time per slide, slides that run long are marked, and the timings go into your notes.
  • Single-file web presentation. Export one .html file that opens anywhere without any program.
EasyWrite · Documents

A word processor that opens and saves Word (.docx), its own .easywrite format, Markdown, HTML and plain text.

  • “/” command menu. Headings, lists, to-dos, quotes, code, callout boxes, collapsible sections, tables, images, page breaks and a table of contents.
  • Markdown as you type. # for a heading, - for a bullet, **bold**, ==highlight==, [toc] and more. Pasted Markdown arrives formatted.
  • Readability analysis. Ateşman (Turkish) or Flesch (English) score, reading time, long sentences and filler words highlighted, without changing your text.
  • Inline maths. Type 1250*12%= and the result is added for you.
  • Focus and typewriter mode, writing goals, a Pomodoro timer, dictation and read-aloud.
  • Templates and paper themes. Letter, CV, meeting notes, report, article, project plan and journal; white, sepia, mint and night paper.

What PowerPoint and Word don't have.

Shared by both apps, from the Platform365 home screen.

  • Document ⇄ presentationTurn an EasyWrite document into a Present deck in one click, or a deck back into an editable document with its notes.
  • Command palettePress Ctrl+K anywhere and type to reach any command.
  • Version historyAutomatic versions while you work and before risky changes, plus named versions you can preview and restore.
  • Search inside everythingSearch the titles and the contents of all your presentations and documents from the home screen.
  • One-click full backupAll decks, documents and versions in a single .p365 file, ready to restore on another device.
  • Smart file openingDrop a .pptx, .docx or .md file on the home screen and it opens in the right app. Light, dark or system theme everywhere.

File formats.

Your files are real Office files inside, so others can open them in PowerPoint, Word, Keynote, Google Slides or LibreOffice.

FormatWhat it is
.presentA valid PowerPoint (.pptx) package plus lossless Present data inside.
.easywriteA valid Word (.docx) package plus lossless EasyWrite data. Rename it to .docx to open it in Word.
.pptxOpened with text formatting, nearly 100 preset shapes, freeform drawings, gradients, picture fills, cropped images, real tables, SmartArt, masters and speaker notes. Saved as clean Office Open XML, with no “repair” warning.
.docxHeadings, character formatting, links, nested lists, to-dos, tables with merged cells, images, quotes, code, callouts, page breaks, page size and margins, both ways.
.md · .html · .txtImport and export.
.p365A full Platform365 backup.

Not carried over from PowerPoint yet: charts (a note box is put in their place), video and audio, animations and transitions, shadow and glow effects, and EMF/WMF images.
